Where ad budget drains away.

More budget doesn't automatically mean more revenue. The causes are usually where standard reports don't look.

Managing by clicks

Cost per click and reach look good, while contribution margin and the share of new customers fall short. What matters is what's actually left per euro of ad spend.

Unreliable measurement

If the platforms report the wrong conversions, the algorithms optimize in the wrong direction. Without reliable tracking, every optimization is guesswork.

A faulty product feed

Missing attributes, disapprovals and neglected titles cost visibility in Shopping and Performance Max, often unnoticed.

Worn-out creatives

Running the same creatives for weeks drives up costs. Without ongoing tests, there's no fresh supply to keep campaigns going.

How we manage campaigns.

FAQ on performance marketing

Short, honest answers. If your question is missing, ask it in the intro call.

What budget makes it worthwhile?

That depends on margin and average order value. In the intro call we run the numbers with your figures to see whether starting pays off.

What do you need to get started?

Access to ad accounts, Merchant Center, Shopify and GA4, plus your margins per product group.

Do we do Meta and Google at the same time?

The channels do different jobs: Meta creates demand, Google serves it. We look at both together so budgets don't cannibalize each other.

What's the difference between ROAS and contribution margin?

ROAS relates ad spend to revenue without accounting for margin, returns or shipping costs. That's why we also manage by contribution margin, so growth also means profit.

How long until campaigns take effect?

First signals usually appear after a few weeks. Reliable conclusions need several weeks of runtime and enough data. We agree in advance how we'll measure success.

How do you deal with inaccurate tracking?

We first check whether the numbers hold up. If they don't, we fix the tracking before shifting budgets, so optimization isn't based on false signals.

Do you make the creatives too?

Ad copy and visuals from your existing imagery, yes. For new photos or videos, we'll tell you what's needed.

How do you report?

Monthly in plain language: what happened, why, and what we'll change next. In between, you can reach us directly.
