Where conversion gets lost.

When visitors come but don't buy, the cause is rarely in a single place. Usually many small hurdles add up.

Product pages don't convince

Missing information, weak images or unclear variants make visitors hesitate before adding to cart.

The path to the product is too long

Navigation and search decide whether visitors find what they're looking for or give up after a few clicks.

The checkout scares people off

Unexpected shipping costs, too many fields or missing payment methods are common reasons for dropping off just before purchase.

Mobile is treated as an afterthought

Most traffic comes from smartphones. If the store is only shrunk down there instead of designed for it, you're leaving revenue on the table.

How we work.

We work through hypotheses, not matters of taste. Every change has a rationale, a metric and an evaluation.

FAQ on conversion optimization

Short, honest answers. If your question is missing, ask it in the intro call.

What's in the audit?

A prioritized list with finding, screenshot and a concrete proposal, sorted by effort and expected impact.

Do you also implement the measures?

Yes, directly in the Shopify theme. Whoever writes the audit also builds the changes.

How do we know if it's working?

Before implementation, we define which metric should move, and we evaluate it afterwards.

What is a good conversion rate?

That depends on industry, price level and traffic source. That's why we compare your store with its own history rather than with generic averages.

Do you need a lot of traffic for tests?

For reliable A/B tests, yes. With less traffic, we rely on analysis and well-founded improvements, and evaluate before and after.

What role does load time play?

A big one. Slow pages increase the bounce rate, especially on smartphones. That's why we check load time as part of the audit.

How quickly will we see results?

Initial improvements often take effect right after implementation. They can be reliably evaluated after a period defined in advance.

What does a CRO project cost?

That depends on the scope. After the audit you know which measures are worth it, and you get a quote with a fixed scope.
